# Fathom Financial Policy (Excerpt)

## Purpose
This document defines how Fathom handles employee reimbursements, vendor invoices, and record-keeping. It also clarifies approval limits and retention periods.

## Reimbursements
Employees must submit receipts within **30 days** of purchase using the Finance Portal. Claims older than 60 days require VP Finance approval.

### Eligible Expenses
- Domestic travel: airfare, train, taxi, hotel, meals
- Work equipment: keyboards, mice, cables (≤ SAR 500 without prior approval)
- Conference fees with manager pre-approval

### Ineligible Expenses
- Personal entertainment
- Family travel add-ons
- Traffic fines

## Approval Limits
- Line Manager: up to **SAR 5,000**
- Department Head: up to **SAR 25,000**
- VP Finance: above **SAR 25,000**

## Vendor Invoices
Vendors must email invoices to **ap@fathom.example** in PDF. Invoices must include vendor name, tax number, PO number, and bank IBAN. Standard payment terms are **Net 30**.

## Record Retention
Receipts and invoices are retained for **7 years** per compliance policy.
